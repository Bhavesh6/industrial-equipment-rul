Rule-Based Prognostic Formulation & Confidence Bounds

Health Index ($H$): $H = 1 - \sqrt{\sum w_i \left(\frac{x_i - \mu_i}{\sigma_i}\right)^2} \times 0.12$, bounded in $[0.0, 1.0]$.

Remaining Useful Life ($RUL$): $\text{RUL} = L_0 \times \frac{H - H_{\text{fail}}}{H_0 - H_{\text{fail}}}$, with $L_0 = 10.0\text{ hrs}$, $H_{\text{fail}} = 0.70$, $H_0 = 1.00$.

Uncertainty Bound (95% CI): $\text{Interval} = \text{RUL} \pm 1.96 \times \sigma_{\text{RUL}}$, where $\sigma_{\text{RUL}} = \frac{L_0}{H_0 - H_{\text{fail}}} \times \sigma_H \approx \pm 1.63\text{ hrs}$.
