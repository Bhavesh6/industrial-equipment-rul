Prognostic Equations & 95% Confidence Bounds

1. Health Index ($H$):
$H = 1 - \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n w_i \left(\frac{x_i - \mu_i}{\sigma_i}\right)^2} \times 0.12$, bounded in $[0.0, 1.0]$.

2. Remaining Useful Life ($RUL$):
$\text{RUL} = L_0 \times \frac{H - H_{\text{fail}}}{H_0 - H_{\text{fail}}}$, with $L_0 = 10.0\text{ hrs}$, $H_{\text{fail}} = 0.70$, $H_0 = 1.00$.

3. Uncertainty Bounds (95% CI):
$\text{Interval} = \text{RUL} \pm 1.96 \times \sigma_{\text{RUL}}$, where $\sigma_{\text{RUL}} \approx \pm 1.63\text{ hrs}$.

End-to-End System Architecture

Physical Layer: RS-380 DC motor (12V, 6000 RPM) powered via 3S LiPo battery, monitored with dual current sensing (system and motor branches), temperature, and vibration.

IIoT Edge Node: ESP32 microcontroller acquiring analog/digital telemetry at 50 Hz, windowing statistics, and publishing JSON payloads over Wi-Fi (MQTT/HTTP) at 2 Hz.

Prognostics Layer: Baseline-normalized RMS Mahalanobis-like health scoring with 95% statistical prediction intervals and SHAP-like feature contributions.

Interface: Single-page responsive SCADA console with real-time digital twin visualization, root cause radar diagnostics, and AI-assisted maintenance playbooks.
